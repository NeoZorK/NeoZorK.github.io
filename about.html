About

Rostyslav Shcherbyna

Rostyslav Shcherbyna is an independent researcher and engineer. He started as a system administrator, project manager and QA team lead, moved into trading on US equity markets, spent nine years in analytics as a co-partner at Filodema Analytics, and then three years as a quantitative researcher at SmartTrader.

He now works independently under NeoZorK Research across time series in many domains, econophysics, AI agents and local language-model infrastructure, verification and reproducibility, knowledge engines and data infrastructure. The approach rests on pre-registration, out-of-sample testing and the equal publication of negative results. The mission: help build innovation for humanity.
